# Addendum Press Release: Minerva Alcaraz Joyería — Checkout Individualization, Atelier Pickup & ERP Integration

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

## Minerva Alcaraz Joyería Enhances Customer Security and Atelier Operations with New Individual Checkout Protocols and ERP Integration

**SAN MIGUEL DE ALLENDE, MX — May 19, 2026** — Minerva Alcaraz Joyería, the leading high-luxury Mexican jewelry brand, today announced a series of operational and digital upgrades to its e-commerce experience. The updates introduce an individual checkout protocol for premium items, mandatory client registration to ensure security, and a refined appointment ritual for product pickup at its flagship Atelier in San Miguel de Allende.

To maintain the highest level of craftsmanship and security, the brand now requires all patrons to register an account before proceeding to acquisition. The checkout process has been enhanced to allow customization per item—including custom gift boxes, engraved text, and personalized messages—followed by an individualized payment gateway redirect via Mercado Pago. This ensures that every high-value piece is processed and tracked independently. Furthermore, the physical Atelier in San Miguel de Allende will now operate appointments exclusively for the pickup and delivery of purchased items, ensuring dedicated, individualized customer service.

All online operations are now dynamically synced with the brand’s custom ERP system. Once a client proceeds to the payment link, the transaction is registered, inventory stock is automatically decremented by SKU, CRM details are updated, and the order is routed to a real-time Kanban logistics board.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Why is account registration mandatory before purchasing?
Mandatory registration ensures that all acquisitions are verified and securely linked to the correct patron's profile. This also registers the client in "THE CIRCLE" loyalty program, where they automatically earn points for their purchases (1 point per $100 MXN) to unlock exclusive benefits.

### 2. Why are payments processed individually per product?
Due to the high-value and custom nature of our artisanal jewelry, each piece undergoes independent handling, packaging, and shipping preparation. Processing payments individually ensures secure tracking, precise stock updates, and cleaner tax invoicing for each custom piece.

### 3. What customizations are available during checkout?
Clients can request dedicated premium boxes, custom gift cards, special character engravings, and customized greeting messages for each item. These details are captured in the checkout form and saved directly with the order record.

### 4. How does the Atelier appointment system work?
Atelier visits are now reserved exclusively for the hand-delivery of purchased pieces. Customers can book an appointment slot at our San Miguel de Allende boutique. The system utilizes real-time scheduling with automatic overlap prevention to guarantee that each client has undivided attention from our curators. The page also features interactive maps and operating hours.

### 5. How does the system sync with the ERP and CRM?
When a client clicks the Mercado Pago link for an item:
*   The purchase is logged in the ERP as a pending or completed sale.
*   The inventory stock for the specific product SKU is decremented.
*   The customer's profile is created or updated in the CRM directory.
*   The order is placed on a visual drag-and-drop Kanban board for artisanal tracking.
*   Circle points are calculated and credited to the patron's account.
